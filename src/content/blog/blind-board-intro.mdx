Analytics, or the eye test? The eye test, or analytics? The answer is obviously
both. It's a silly debate that NBA draftniks like to have. But what if video
didn't exist and you only had the data? How well would you do? That's the
thought experiment behind [the Blind Board](/games/blind-board).

There are a few different ways to play. The **Daily Challenge** shows you two
players who were available at an actual draft spot. Using only their stats,
choose the one who went on to have the better career. Here's one from the 2012
draft. Pick one to see who they were.

In **Historical Draft**, you replay the draft history for a given team or a
given draft slot. Think the Sacramento Kings' GMs have been so bad that you
could beat them without the help of video? Replay every Kings pick for any
stretch from 2011 to 2025. Or maybe you think you'd do better with late
second-rounders than most GMs. Go ahead and replay the 52nd overall pick from
each of the last five drafts.
