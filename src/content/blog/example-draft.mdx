`draft: true` keeps a post out of the build, out of the listing, and out of the RSS
feed. Flip it to `false` (or delete the line) to publish.

Required frontmatter: `title`, `description`, `date`, `kind`. Optional: `project`
(a slug from `src/data/projects.ts`, which links the post to that tool) and
`draft`.

`kind` must be one of **Finding**, **Method**, or **Notes** — the build fails
otherwise, which is the point.
